Forces of Nature's Transformation is an ongoing photo project documenting the intersection of nature and urban life. This project takes its name from the 2024 album of the same name by Boston crust punk band Lifeless Dark. The lyrics of the song forecasts nature's revenge on the powerful who extract from it for their personal gain. The theme of the lyrics are of vengance, but, for me, they also inspire hope. Hope in nature's resilance and that one day, when humans are gone, nature continues. Forces of Nature's Transformation is the collection of the observed signs of that idea.

Each image on this page links to a work of literature that directly or indirectly inspired this series.
